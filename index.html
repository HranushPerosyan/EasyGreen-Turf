FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our artificial grass solutions.

How long does artificial grass last? +
Our premium artificial grass is designed to last between 15-20 years with proper care. We back our products with an industry-leading 15+ year warranty, ensuring you get long-lasting value for your investment. The UV-stabilized fibers resist fading and wear, keeping your lawn looking fresh year after year.
Is artificial grass safe for children and pets? +
Absolutely! Our artificial turf is made from non-toxic, lead-free materials that are completely safe for children and pets. It's soft underfoot, hypoallergenic, and free from harmful chemicals. Pets love playing on it, and cleanup is easy — just rinse with water.
How is the artificial grass installed? +
Our professional installation process includes: site preparation, base installation with proper drainage, laying the turf, securing seams, and adding infill for a natural feel. Most residential installations are completed in just one day. Our team handles everything from start to finish.
Does artificial grass get hot in summer? +
Like any surface exposed to direct sunlight, artificial grass can warm up. However, our premium turf features advanced cooling technology that keeps it significantly cooler than standard artificial grass. A quick spray of water on hot days instantly cools the surface, making it comfortable to walk on.
What maintenance does artificial grass require? +
Minimal maintenance is required! Simply brush the grass occasionally to keep fibers upright, remove debris like leaves, and rinse with water when needed. No mowing, watering, fertilizing, or pesticides required. This saves you significant time and money compared to natural grass.
Can artificial grass be installed on any surface? +
Yes! Our artificial grass can be installed on various surfaces including soil, concrete, decking, rooftops, and even indoor spaces. Our team assesses your specific site and recommends the best installation method to ensure optimal performance and longevity.
Do you offer free quotes and consultations? +
Yes, we provide completely free, no-obligation quotes and on-site consultations. Our experts will visit your property, assess your needs, measure the area, and provide a detailed quote with various turf options to suit your budget and requirements.
